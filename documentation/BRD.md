# Business Requirements Document (BRD)
**Project Name:** TheTribe  
**Date:** 2026-01-15  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
**TheTribe** is a private, invite-only web platform designed to foster a high-value mentorship ecosystem for men. The platform aims to connect experienced mentors with motivated learners in a structured, exclusive environment. Unlike open social networks, TheTribe prioritizes quality over quantity, ensuring a focused space for growth through structured training, live mentoring, and meaningful community interaction.

## 2. Business Objectives
*   **Establish a High-Quality Community:** Curate a network of high-performing individuals through a strict invite-only mechanism.
*   **Deliver Structured Value:** Provide organized training paths and resources rather than just an open feed.
*   **Facilitate Direct Mentorship:** Enable real-time interaction between mentors and members via live sessions.
*   **Scalable Foundation:** Build a web-first MVP that establishes the brand and user base before expanding into a full application ecosystem.

## 3. Target Audience
*   **Primary User (Member):** Men seeking personal and professional growth, accountability, and guidance. They value exclusivity and are willing to commit to self-improvement.
*   **Secondary User (Mentor):** Experienced individuals looking to give back, build a following of dedicated students, or monetize their expertise (future state).
*   **Demographics:** Men aged 21-50, career-focused, self-improvement oriented.

## 4. Value Proposition
*   **Exclusivity:** "Iron sharpens iron." Access is a privilege, not a right, ensuring a higher caliber of networking.
*   **Clarity:** Structured pathways for growth vs. the noise of traditional social media.
*   **Access:** Direct lines to mentors who are often inaccessible elsewhere.

## 5. Success Metrics (KPIs)
*   **Invitation Conversion Rate:** % of sent invites that result in active accounts.
*   **Weekly Active Users (WAU):** Measure of consistent engagement with content or community.
*   **Content Completion Rate:** % of users finishing started training modules.
*   **Live Session Attendance:** Average number of attendees per live mentoring event.
*   **Retention Rate:** % of users active after months 1, 3, and 6.

## 6. Assumptions & Risks
### Assumptions
*   Invite-only scarcity will drive demand and perceived value.
*   Users prefer a dedicated platform over Facebook groups or Discord servers for this specific purpose.
*   Mentors are willing to produce exclusive content for this platform.

### Risks
*   **Engagement Drop-off:** If content is not updated frequently, users may churn.
*   **Vetting Quality:** If invites are too loose, community quality dilutes; if too strict, growth stalls.
*   **Platform Fatigue:** Users are already on many platforms; switching costs are high.

## 7. Future Monetization Strategy
*   **Phase 1 (MVP):** Free access (growth & beta testing).
*   **Phase 2 (Premium Membership):** Monthly subscription for access to premium courses, exclusive live Q&As, and archive access.
*   **Phase 3 (Marketplace):** One-off purchases for specialized mentorship programs or events.
